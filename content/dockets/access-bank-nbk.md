## The story so far

National Bank of Kenya was set up in 1968 as a state-owned bank and passed to KCB Group in 2019. In March 2024 KCB agreed to sell all of it to Access Bank PLC, a subsidiary of Nigeria's Access Holdings, which already owned a Kenyan lender, Access Bank (Kenya). The sale needed three Kenyan approvals. The Competition Authority of Kenya cleared it in October 2024 on condition that Access keep at least 80% of NBK's 1,384 employees for a year after completion and retain all 316 employees of Access Bank (Kenya). The Central Bank of Kenya approved the acquisition on 4 April 2025 under section 13(4) of the Banking Act, and the Cabinet Secretary for the National Treasury approved it on 10 April 2025 under section 9 of the Banking Act. As part of the deal, certain NBK assets and liabilities moved to KCB Bank Kenya.

The sale completed at the end of May 2025, and the two banks Access now owned in Kenya carried on separately while the group planned their integration. That integration is what this docket tracks. Access Bank (Kenya) is transferring all of its assets and liabilities to NBK. The Central Bank approved the transfer on 17 August 2026 and the Cabinet Secretary on 21 September 2026.

## Why it matters

A transfer of the whole business is the usual way a Kenyan banking group consolidates after an acquisition, because it avoids running two licences, two boards and two sets of regulatory returns for one group. Under section 9 of the Banking Act, a transfer of a bank's assets and liabilities to another institution needs approval from both the Central Bank of Kenya and the Cabinet Secretary for the National Treasury, which is why this docket has two gates and both are now cleared. For customers, the practical effect is that accounts, deposits and loans at Access Bank (Kenya) move to NBK once the transfer takes effect.

It is also one of two Nigerian bank consolidations on the Wire this year, alongside Zenith Bank's purchase of Paramount Bank (No. 2 of 2026).

## What to watch

The effective date of the transfer has not been announced. Watch for a public notice setting that date, for the point at which Access Bank (Kenya) customers start banking with NBK, and for whether the combined bank keeps the NBK name.
