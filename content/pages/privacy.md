---
title: Privacy
description: How Docket Wire handles the email address you give it.
---

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics.

If you subscribe, the email address you enter is held by our email provider for one purpose: sending you Docket Wire. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. Every email carries a link to unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time using the contact details on the About page.

If you follow a docket, the list of dockets you follow and the date you last saw each one are kept in your own browser, on your device, so the site can mark what is new. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes them. The site also keeps copies of the pages you open on your device so that you can read them offline.
